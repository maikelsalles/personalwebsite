## Professional Narrative

Maikel Salles is a Senior Front-End Designer and Product Designer with over 15 years of experience creating responsive web and mobile products across enterprise software, payments, fleet management, healthcare, media, and startup environments.

His work sits at the intersection of Design, Product, and Engineering. He translates research, analytics, user needs, and business requirements into user flows, wireframes, prototypes, high-fidelity interfaces, design systems, and production-ready specifications.

He has helped establish a Product Design department, led Design System work for Angular web products and a React Native mobile application, and delivered measurable outcomes in accessibility, delivery efficiency, and product clarity.

## Language to Avoid

- Exaggerated superlatives ("world-class," "revolutionary," "best-in-class") without evidence
- Vague claims ("passionate innovator," "thought leader") without supporting context
- Unqualified metrics — always use "approximately" where the profile uses it
- Generic corporate filler that could apply to any designer
- Informal or inconsistent job titles that conflict with the approved resume

## Unsupported Claims to Avoid

Do not use content that is not in [professional-profile.md](./professional-profile.md) or the latest approved resume, including:

- Specific regulatory fees or financial penalties (e.g., unverified dollar amounts)

- Proficiency percentages or skill bars not supported by evidence
